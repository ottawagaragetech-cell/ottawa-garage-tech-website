Who we are

Ottawa Garage Tech helps homeowners across the National Capital Region keep garage doors safe, balanced, and weather-ready. We are not a national franchise or a general handyman — we focus on garage doors, openers, springs, cables, tracks, and seals, day in and day out.

As a mobile garage door service, we come to your home with the tools and common parts needed for most repairs. That means less downtime when a spring breaks before work, an opener stops responding in cold weather, or a door will not close and your home is not secure.

Ottawa winters are hard on hardware: metal contracts, seals stiffen, and worn springs fail without warning. We factor climate into every recommendation — from bottom seals and weather stripping to insulated door upgrades up to R-18 and preventive tune-ups before the first deep freeze.

Garage door services we provide

Whether you need a quick fix or a full replacement, we handle residential and light commercial garage doors throughout the city and suburbs. Our most common jobs include:

  • Emergency garage door repair — off-track doors, doors that will not close, and urgent safety issues
  • Garage door spring replacement — torsion and extension springs, usually replaced in pairs
  • Garage door opener repair and installation — motors, gears, remotes, and safety sensors
  • Cable and drum repair — frayed cables, slipped drums, and balance problems
  • New garage door installation — insulated doors up to R-18, measure, quote, install, seal, and balance
  • Annual maintenance tune-ups — lubrication, tension checks, and wear inspection
  • Weather sealing — bottom seals, thresholds, and draft reduction

How we work

Every visit follows the same straightforward process. You tell us what happened — a loud bang, a door that reverses, a gap under the seal. We inspect the full system (springs, cables, tracks, rollers, opener, and hardware), explain what we find in plain language, and provide a quote before major repair or installation work begins.

For urgent safety issues — a door off track, hanging unevenly, or unable to secure your home — call or text first. That is the fastest way to reach us, especially evenings and weekends when email is slower.
