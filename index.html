How much does garage door repair cost in Ottawa?

Costs depend on the repair. Spring work is often $220–$380; cable repairs $175–$275; tune-ups from $150. Emergency visits start around $199 before parts. We explain pricing before work starts.

Do you offer same-day garage door service?

Yes, when scheduling allows. For urgent issues — a door that will not close, off-track panels, or a broken spring — call or text (613) 900-6005 for the fastest response.

Should both garage door springs be replaced?

Usually yes. If one torsion or extension spring has failed, the other is often near end of life. Replacing both restores balance and helps prevent a second breakdown soon after.
