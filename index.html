What should I budget for garage door work in Ottawa?

It depends on the fault. One spring $235–$350; both springs $450–$550; cables about $180–$285; tune-ups from $165. Priority safety visits usually start near $215 before parts. We walk you through scope and price before anything major begins.

Can you often fix it the same day I call?

Frequently yes, when scheduling allows. For urgent issues — a door that will not close, off-track panels, or a broken spring — call or text (613) 900-6005 for the fastest response.

Is replacing one spring enough?

Usually replace the pair. If one torsion or extension spring has failed, the mate is often near the end of its cycle. Matching springs keep the door balanced and reduce repeat calls.
