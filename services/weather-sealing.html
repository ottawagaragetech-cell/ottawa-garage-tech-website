Weather sealing

A thin gap under your garage door lets in Ottawa winter air, slush, and rodents. Quality bottom seals and jamb weatherstripping make a noticeable difference in comfort and energy loss.

Sealing only works when the door is aligned — we check contact across the full width and adjust tracks or limits if needed so the new seal wears evenly.

Signs you may need weather sealing

  • Daylight visible under the closed door
  • Snow or water pooling inside the garage
  • Cold drafts near the garage entry
  • Rubber seal is cracked or missing chunks
  • Pests entering under the door

Our process

  1. Measure door width and seal profile
  2. Remove worn seal and clean the retainer
  3. Install new bottom seal or threshold
  4. Replace side and top weatherstrip as quoted

What we handle

  • T-style and U-shaped bottom seals
  • Threshold bars
  • PVC and rubber jamb seal
  • Track tweaks for even contact

Common questions about weather sealing

When is the best time to replace seals?

Late fall before deep cold, or when you notice drafts and daylight under the door.

Will sealing reduce heating costs?

It helps especially if the garage shares a wall with heated space.

Can you fix uneven sealing?

Yes — we align the door so the seal contacts evenly end to end.
