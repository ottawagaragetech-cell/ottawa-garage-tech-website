Signs you may need garage door opener service

  • Motor runs but door does not move
  • Grinding or clicking from the opener head
  • Remotes or wall button unresponsive
  • Door reverses immediately when closing
  • LED lights flash on the opener (error codes)

Our process

  1. Test door balance first — sometimes the door is the real issue
  2. Inspect gears, carriage, sensors, and wiring
  3. Quote repair vs replacement honestly
  4. Program remotes and test auto-reverse safety

Common questions about garage door opener service

My opener hums but the door does not move — why?

Often stripped plastic gears inside the unit. Repair is usually cheaper than full replacement.

Which opener type is quietest?

Belt-drive models are typically quieter than chain-drive — good for bedrooms above the garage.

Can you program my car HomeLink?

Yes, during the same visit when compatible with your opener.
