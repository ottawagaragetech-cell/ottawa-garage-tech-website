Maintenance tune-ups

Most garage door failures give warning signs months earlier — noisy rollers, slow response, or a door that drifts off balance. A yearly tune-up catches those issues before they become emergency calls.

Our maintenance visit is designed for Ottawa homes: we account for salt, cold, and hardware contraction that affects rollers, hinges, and seals.

Tune-ups from $150 — includes inspection, lubrication, and a written summary of findings.

Signs you may need maintenance tune-ups

  • Door is noisy when opening or closing
  • Has been more than 12 months since service
  • Door does not stay open at mid-height
  • Rubber seal is cracked or flat
  • Preparing a rental property or new home purchase

Our process

  1. Visual inspection of springs, cables, tracks, and panels
  2. Lubricate hinges, rollers, and bearing plates
  3. Test auto-reverse and photo-eye sensors
  4. Adjust opener limits if needed and provide a report

What we handle

  • 21-point inspection checklist
  • Hinge and roller lubrication
  • Hardware tightening
  • Safety sensor testing

Common questions about maintenance tune-ups

How often should I service my door?

Once a year is ideal for most Ottawa households — more if heavy daily use.

Is maintenance worth it vs waiting for a break?

Yes — preventing a spring or cable failure avoids higher emergency costs.

Do you lubricate all parts?

We use appropriate lubricants on moving metal parts — not on tracks where grease attracts dirt.
