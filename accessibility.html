Accessibility

Last updated: May 2026

Our commitment

Ottawa Garage Tech is committed to providing a website and service experience that is accessible to all customers, including people with disabilities. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and to comply with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA).

Website features

  • Semantic HTML structure with clear headings, lists, and landmarks.
  • Keyboard navigation for menus, dropdowns, forms, and the back-to-top button.
  • Visible focus indicators on interactive elements.
  • Descriptive alternative text on meaningful images.
  • Sufficient colour contrast for body text and call-to-action buttons.
  • Responsive layout that works on phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • Respects the user’s “reduced motion” system preference.

Service accessibility

If you need accommodations when scheduling or receiving garage door service — for example, communication by text or email instead of phone, or extra time at your home — please tell us when you book. We will do our best to support your needs.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review this site as we add new content and welcome suggestions to make it easier to use.
