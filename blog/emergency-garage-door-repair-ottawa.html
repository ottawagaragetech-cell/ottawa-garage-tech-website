What counts as an emergency garage door problem

Call for urgent service if any of these apply:

  • Door stuck open — the house is exposed overnight; vehicles and belongings are visible from the street
  • Broken spring — a loud bang, then the door will not lift or feels extremely heavy (see our broken spring guide)
  • Off-track or crooked door — panels binding in the rails; forcing the opener can bend the door or strip gears
  • Cable snapped or frayed — cables under tension can whip; stop using the opener
  • Door will not close and sensors look fine — leave it secured another way until inspected (won't-close troubleshooting)
  • Opener runs but the door barely moves — often balance, spring, or carriage failure, not “just a remote”

A squeaky door or a remote that needs new batteries is maintenance — not an emergency. A door that will not secure the garage is.

Safe first steps before the tech arrives

  1. Stop using the opener if the door is crooked, grinding, or much heavier than usual
  2. Keep people and pets clear of the door path and the spring area near the ceiling
  3. Do not adjust torsion springs or cables — high tension; DIY spring work is a common injury
  4. If the door is stuck open, park a vehicle to block the opening only if it is safe to do so, and avoid standing under a partially open door
  5. Note what happened — bang, grinding, reverse at the floor, storm, ice — short details help us bring the right parts

If you must disconnect the opener for manual operation, follow the manufacturer’s emergency-release instructions. Do not yank the red cord repeatedly on a door that feels unbalanced.

Why Ottawa doors fail suddenly

Cold snaps, freeze-thaw, and road salt speed up spring fatigue and cable wear. Busy family schedules in Barrhaven, Kanata Lakes, Orleans, and Nepean mean more open-close cycles per year. A door that “felt fine yesterday” can fail the morning after the first hard frost — that is normal for springs near end of life, not bad luck alone.

If the door has been noisy, heavy, or uneven for weeks, book maintenance before it becomes an after-hours emergency. Opener strain and worn rollers often show up first as sound — see noisy garage door fixes.
